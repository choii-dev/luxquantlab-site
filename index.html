About the Lab

A lab for curious, disciplined research.

Lux Quant Lab is an independent research lab focused on quantitative trading research, alongside exploration of other quantitative projects.

The goal is to ask clear questions, test ideas against data, and learn from results — without presenting unverified backtests or research as a promise of future performance.
